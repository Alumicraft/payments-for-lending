## Source findings and pending decisions

- Accounting audit added: existing `ensure_lending_accounting_defaults` can fill missing Loan Product income and accrual mappings with the service/fee income account, and principal/interest/penalty receivables with the loans receivable account. These existing defaults require live configuration and journal review; their presence is not proof that accounting is correct. DCR schedules ACH tasks, while native Lending accrual/demand jobs and their execution still need verification.

- Curtailment is fixed at 1% of the original invoice starting with payment 13. The current controller still uses a percentage of remaining principal. Confirm the immutable invoice source and fee treatment before changing it; the schedule's loan amount can differ from the final invoice.
- The current preview uses principal × annual rate / 1200, and a fixed number of monthly periods. Its default horizon is 12 periods. The custom schedule also uses monthly interest rather than dated daily accrual. Agree the 360-day convention, endpoint dates, and horizon before changing accounting behavior.
- The Dealer Flooring Loan Payoff print derives interest owed from forecast total interest minus interest paid. That can retain future forecast interest on an early payoff. Its total also uses principal collected, so confirm whether this document is a settlement summary or a payoff quote before replacing its amounts with native Lending balances. This finding does not establish that native loan closure collects future interest.
- Application defaults currently take the HBR quoted amount. The reviewed DCR Purchase Invoice hook repairs expense accounts and contains no final-invoice reconciliation into the loan; hosted customizations are unverified. Confirm the invoice amount, funding date, fee treatment, and revised-signature policy together.
- The ACH packet already prints Loan Application.monthly_insurance_amount. This batch provisions the field if absent and verifies a populated value renders; an empty amount or existing site customization still needs a staff and hosted check.
- Factory packets retain the existing four document requirements. Submission is now quiet, and a deliberate staff send uses the persisted assignment and requires write/email permissions. Plant names, recipient contacts, and the minimum acceptable packet still need DCR confirmation.
